The error occurs because when you check in at `15:00` (3:00 PM) and check out at `00:30` (12:30 AM), JavaScript reads both times on the *same calendar date*, making 12:30 AM look like it happened *before* 3:00 PM. To fix this, the script has been updated to automatically detect if the check-out time falls on the **next day** and shifts the date calculation forward by 24 hours. Additionally, the application now automatically defaults the dropdown selector to the **current active calendar month** right when you visit or refresh the page.
